1"""rover_wizard.motion.gps_heading - the vehicle's true heading from a straight run, for a compass calibration. 

2 

3A single GPS can't tell which way a standing vehicle points, but while it drives straight forward its course over 

4ground is its heading. The gyro (independent of the compass) then follows any turn up to the moment the vehicle 

5stands still again: each GPS course reading plus the turn since then is an estimate of the final heading, and their 

6circular mean is the result. Their spread shows how straight the run was. Sideways drift (a boat in a current or wind) 

7makes the course differ from the heading: the result is only as good as the run. 

8 

9Pure functions only: no vehicle access, no UI, no clock. 

10"""

23# [m/s] slower than this, a GPS course is mostly noise 

24MIN_GPS_SPEED = 0.5 

25# at least this many course readings while moving (a few seconds of driving) 

26MIN_COURSE_READINGS = 10 

27# [deg] the course estimates may spread this much (one standard deviation): more means the run wasn't straight 

28MAX_COURSE_SPREAD_DEG = 5.0 

29# [m/s] at the end the vehicle must stand still: the calibration needs the heading it has then

89def _circular_mean_and_spread(angles: list) -> tuple: 

90 """Mean [deg, 0..360] and circular standard deviation [deg] of angles [rad].""" 

91 sine = sum(math.sin(angle) for angle in angles) / len(angles) 

92 cosine = sum(math.cos(angle) for angle in angles) / len(angles) 

93 length = min(1.0, math.hypot(sine, cosine)) 

94 # circular standard deviation: sqrt(-2 ln R) 

95 spread = math.degrees(math.sqrt(-2 * math.log(length))) if length > 0 else math.inf 

96 return math.degrees(math.atan2(sine, cosine)) % FULL_CIRCLE_DEG, spread
